Read this first. This tool is a screening and research aid, not investment advice, and it is not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything. Scores are arithmetic on published PSX data using weights you control — they are a starting point for your own reading of the annual report, not a substitute for it. PSX exposes only four years of Sales / Profit / EPS, so balance-sheet risk (debt, gearing, ROE, cash flow) is not captured here and must be checked in the company's own accounts before any decision. Data is supplied by PSX / Capital Stake and may be delayed or restated.
